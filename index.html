Bioprocess & process engineering

Practical engineering around fermentation, process equipment and utilities, with attention to measurability, reproducibility and maintainability.

  • Fermenters and pilot-scale equipment
  • Temperature, pH, DO and feed control
  • CIP/SIP, process heating and equipment specification

Industrial automation & instrumentation

Control and measurement solutions for process installations, including upgrades to existing systems where replacement of the complete installation is unnecessary.

  • PLC programming and I/O integration
  • pH, DO, temperature, pressure and other instrumentation
  • Modbus, gateways and process logging

Technical software & integration

Small, maintainable software tools and interfaces for industrial environments, especially where machines, process data and business systems need to connect.

  • Batch logging and reporting
  • Embedded and Linux-based gateways
  • ERP and machine-data integration

Typical projects

Assignments are usually compact and technically focused. Examples include:

  • Fermenter instrumentation and control, including pH, DO and temperature
  • Steam, SIP and process-heating questions
  • PLC batch logging, data export and technical reporting
  • Industrial gateways between machines and ERP systems
  • Selection and specification of pumps, valves, sensors and heat exchangers

How Biodys works

Direct contact, short lines and a pragmatic approach. Depending on the assignment, work may consist of analysis, design, prototyping, supplier evaluation, implementation support or troubleshooting.
